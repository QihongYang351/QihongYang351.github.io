# Moments captions

All nine photographs and their month/year dates come from the user's desktop folder. Dates are intentionally kept at month precision; entries with the same month retain their configured order.

Captions are maintained independently in `content/moments.toml` and `content_zh/moments.toml`. `sort_date` controls newest-first ordering, and `position` controls CSS framing without changing the original photograph. Clicking a photograph opens the complete original.
